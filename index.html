01

CSV Boka Northern Ocean

Multi-scope vessel modification engineering Sep 2025 → ongoing

Deck reconfiguration and system modifications on an SPS-Code subsea construction vessel, driven by the installation of new ROV systems — all designed to stay inside the vessel’s existing statutory and class envelope.

  • Gangway access rearrangement (P&S) — platform relocation, new deck openings, taut-wire grillage removal, walkways and handrails.
  • PDU container installation — two containers on starboard side; seafastening, cable trays and deck strength verification.
  • 400 t crane bucket relocation — hoist-line buckets moved; bumper protection around doghouse, ladder and refuelling pedestal.
  • Closing bulwarks & freeing ports — hinged freeing ports at pilot access; ICLL freeing-port evaluation and loadline plan updates.
  • Project office → ROV control room — fire safety, escape, HVAC and electrical SLD engineering per SPS Code and SOLAS.
  • Fire pump relocation — sea chest sizing, NPSH and dynamics calculations; piping, structural and electrical workshop drawings.
  • Gastight cofferdam over Ex-rated battery room — scantlings, F&G modifications, ventilation and hazardous-area plans.
  • Mooring fittings, pilot walkway, whipline platform and helideck plan (CAP 437) updates.

Delivered with clash-free FEED 3D local models (IGES), full Class/Flag documentation and direct liaison with Bureau Veritas through approval.

03

DSV Jana 201 — roll reduction

Independent hydrodynamic consultancy Feb 2026

A diving support vessel under construction showing unusually high roll and pitch in moderate seas. An independent review established a baseline and options for motion control.

  • Linear diffraction analysis: roll RAOs, natural roll and pitch periods, roll stiffness and damping (bilge keel, viscous, nonlinear GZ).
  • Review of hull form, stability, shipyard hydrodynamic predictions and loading conditions.
  • Operability assessment including moonpool and heading effects.
  • Recommendations: loading for GM and roll stiffness, heading selection, bilge keel optimisation, passive stabilisation (flume, U-tank, controlled-flooding tanks).

Deliverable: consolidated technical report on hydrodynamic review and motion stabilisation.

04

National Geographic Gemini

Roll resonance & passenger comfort Jul 2025

An expedition vessel whose natural roll period sat close to the prevailing wave energy — large motions even in calm conditions. The task: de-tune the resonance.

  • Panel, mass and damping model; roll RAO, added mass and inertia, damping coefficients and free-surface effects.
  • Alternative loading conditions: GM variation, roll gyradius, draft increase and partial tank filling.
  • Feasibility of using existing full-breadth double-bottom tanks as passive stabilisers.
  • Bilge keel modification and passive/active stabiliser options.

Deliverables: operating procedure for roll reduction and a stabilisation feasibility study with bilge keel modification plan.
