Engineering and Consultancy

UK Projects

Hydrodynamic, stability and modification work for leading expedition, offshore and decarbonisation operators.

National Geographic, Lindblad Expeditions

Roll Resonance and Passenger Comfort

A hydrodynamic study to de-tune roll resonance on an expedition vessel.

Project Details
NG Gemini: hydrodynamic model, loading conditions to move the natural roll period, existing tanks as passive stabilisers, bilge keel changes and stabiliser options.
JANA Marine Services

Diving Support Vessel Motion Study

Independent roll reduction and motion control study for a diving support vessel under construction.

Project Details
DSV Jana 201: linear diffraction analysis, natural periods and damping, operability with moonpool and heading effects, and stabilisation options.
Maersk and Njord

eSAIL Wind-Assisted Propulsion

Stability, class approval and foundation fatigue for wind-assisted propulsion on product tankers.

Project Details
Intact and damage stability, WASP criteria, inclining experiments and draft surveys, class comment closure, and a preliminary stability booklet. The foundation method became the SMATECH 2026 fatigue paper.
Draft Increase

Feasibility Studies

Structural and stability assessments taken through to class submission.

Project Details
Salt Wind Explorer and Berge Vinson.
Boskalis

Offshore Construction Vessel Modifications

Ten modification packages for new ROV systems, designed within the vessel’s class and statutory envelope, with Bureau Veritas approval.

Project Details
Boka Northern Ocean: gangway access, PDU containers, 400 t crane bucket and bumpers, bulwarks and freeing ports (ICLL), ROV control room (SPS Code, SOLAS), fire pump relocation, mooring fittings, pilot walkway, battery-room cofferdam and helideck plan.

Drydock Gates
