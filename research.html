An Integrated Approach to Energy Optimisation: Optimal Steaming with Controllable Pitch Propulsion

Finds a vessel’s optimum power demand for a given speed and sea state, then the propeller RPM and pitch combination with the lowest fuel use. A case study on a 40 m wind farm service vessel showed a large cut in fuel per nautical mile.

Lead author and presenter, with M R Kaushik Shiva and Dr Sudheesh Ramadasan

Ensuring Fatigue Life of WAPS Foundations

An ABS-compliant fatigue assessment for wind-assisted propulsion foundations, combining aerodynamic block loading with probabilistic wave loads. On a 24 m eSAIL foundation, the existing structure carried the sail for 25 years without reinforcement.

Lead author, with Bikash Gupta and Dr Sudheesh Ramadasan

MARINE 2025, EdinburghJune 2025

How to Use JoRes Ship-Scale Open Data for CFD Validation?

Introduces the JoRes open ship-scale validation dataset of seven vessels and suggests how best to use it to validate CFD methods.

Co-author, with Dmitry Ponkratov and Miles Wheeler of Siemens Digital Industries Software
University of SouthamptonMSc Thesis, September 2024

Numerical Investigation of the Performance of Ships with Wind-Assisted Propulsion

A coupled hull and Flettner-rotor CFD study in Star-CCM+, showing speed gains of about 24% at low speed and 5.5% at design speed.

Supervised by Prof Tahsin Tezdogan
Open Data

JoRes4 Tug Digital Hull Geometry

I converted a dry-dock 3D laser scan of the JoRes4 tug into a clean, CFD-ready hull geometry, and found and corrected a propeller asymmetry against the as-designed geometry.

It was published as open validation data with co-authors from the JoRes project, the University of Southampton and Wärtsilä.

Visit jores.net

Maritime Robotics

A Robot Steered Without GPS

I chose Maritime Robotics as an elective out of interest. I assembled a floating robot, wrote its code and operated it, steering it to a target and along a set path with two thrusters and no GPS or position sensors, by tuning it until its actual motion matched the commands.
