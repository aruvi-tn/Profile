Behind the Demo

How the Real Module Works

Pick the Crane and Load Chart

The operator selects the crane and its load chart, the cargo and the lift type, at sea or in harbour.

Check Before Lifting

Stability is recomputed with the cargo on the hook. If heel or trim is outside the limits, anti-heeling fills tanks to bring them back.

Plan for Loss of Load

The sudden-loss-of-load case is checked against the counter-ballast, with live draft and tank readings from the vessel.

This page uses a simplified illustrative vessel: 12,000 t displacement, GM 3.0 m, wall-sided small-angle method.
