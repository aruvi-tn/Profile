Innovate UK, Smart Shipping Acceleration Fund

SUMA EFE

AI/ML software that cuts fuel and carbon on vessels with controllable pitch propellers.

Masters of controllable pitch propeller vessels often run them like fixed-pitch ones, because the best pitch for the conditions is hard to find. SUMA EFE reads wind, waves and current, works out the resistance and power demand, and recommends the pitch and engine RPM that use the least fuel.

  • From Idea to Product. I took an early Cybermarine idea and led it into SUMA EFE: architecture, technical features, calculation logic, interface and specification. I led the developers through testing and delivery.
  • AI/ML. I introduced the machine-learning approach under the Innovate UK project.
  • On Board. I was on RV Prince Madog for the inspection and the installation, and later updated the software remotely.
  • Approved. I led SUMA EFE through Lloyd’s Register type approval.

Heavy Lift, Anti-Heeling and Draft Survey

Plan a crane lift before it happens.

On a heavy-lift offshore vessel, the main cranes were run separately from the loadicator. I added three modules to CyberMaster3D and connected them to live draft and tank sensors, so the crew see the whole vessel on one screen and plan each lift before it happens.

  • My Part. The logic, the crane centre-of-gravity algorithm and the interface. I led the software team to delivery.
  • Before the Lift. The crew see the effect on stability and get the ballast calculated, including a sudden loss of load.
  • Draft Survey. Locates unknown weights, including flooding, from measured drafts.

Grain Stability

G-Mod

A grain stability version of CyberMaster.

When class amended the grain stability rules for bulk carriers, owners did not want to replace the loadicators already on board. I led Cybermarine’s grain stability work: I studied the amendment, built tools to calculate the amended moments and developed G-Mod.

  • How It Works. Imports condition reports from the vessel’s existing loadicator, rebuilds each loading condition, checks it against the amended rules and produces a new report.
  • Result. Owners stay compliant without new hardware.
